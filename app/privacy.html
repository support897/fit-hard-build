Fit Hard Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

Fit Hard is a kind fitness companion. The app does not show ads and does not track you. Everything you enter is stored on your device. If you choose a background photo in Settings, Appearance, that photo is stored the same way, under your account, and only you can remove it. If you choose to sign in, a copy is also stored in your own account (below) so the app can follow you between devices.

What the app stores

Your setup answers, meal plans, recipes, treats, gym routines, peptide tracking entries, habits, to do items and check ins are stored on your device so the app can show them back to you. Photos you add are looked at on your device and are not uploaded.

Calendar import: if you bring a calendar in, the app reads the file or link you give it once and copies the events into your calendar here. Your other calendar is never changed. Imported events are stored like the rest of your data, under your account, and stay yours.

Accounts

Signing in is your choice, and the app works fully without it. If you sign in, your app data is stored in the app's own Google Firebase project (Firestore, in Sydney, Australia) under your account only, so it can follow you between devices. You can sign in with an email and a password you choose, with Google, or with Apple. Your password is held by the sign-in service (Google Firebase); the app itself never stores it. Your email address is used only for signing you in. Nobody else can read your stored data, and it is never sold or shared.

Health information note

Fit Hard is not a doctor and gives no medical advice. It only tracks what you enter, including amounts you record for products you already use. Talk to your doctor about any health question.

Deleting your data

You can remove the app data at any time by clearing the app storage on your device or removing the app. If you have an account, Settings has a Delete my account button: it deletes your stored cloud copy, deletes your sign-in, and wipes the copy on the device you are using. You can also download a full copy of your data from Settings at any time.
