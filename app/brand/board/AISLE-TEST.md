# Fit App — Aisle Test (the Lab's board condition, discharged 14 Oct 2026)

The condition on the Fit board's PASS (Innovation Lab design
read, 14 Oct): the record must carry the aisle test — the
fitness shelf as the research found it, against this board's
warm cream-and-amber world. Evidence source: Store Review's
logo research, ~/workspace/store-review/LOGO-RESEARCH-2026-10-14.md
(Category 7 — Fitness tracking; live store artwork + chart
data, crawled Oct 2026).

## The shelf as found

- **MyFitnessPal** — royal blue tile, white jumping-person
  mark. The revenue king of fitness (~$310M revenue 2025,
  ~140M Play installs, #2 Top Grossing H&F US). The HUMAN
  lane: energy, not punishment.
- **Strava** — saturated orange, white peak glyph. ~140M
  Play installs, #4 Top Grossing H&F US. Hard geometry,
  performance register.
- **Fitbod** — magenta tile, badge mark. #10 Top Grossing
  H&F US, 4.8M Play installs. Saturated, serious.
- **Strong / Hevy and the gym crowd** — barbell marks,
  dark/neon performance styling; Strong out-installed by
  Hevy (8.4M installs) in the same hard lane.
- Category read, verbatim from the research: "Fitness
  splits into the human lane (MyFitnessPal's jumper — the
  revenue king) and the hard-geometry lane (Strava's peak,
  Strong's barbell, Fitbod's badge). Colour is doing heavy
  lifting: orange, royal blue, magenta — all saturated,
  all owned, none of them black-with-neon."

## Against this board

- **Tile/ground:** every leader sits on a SATURATED solid
  (royal blue, orange, magenta) or dark performance chrome.
  Fit's ground is warm CREAM #FFF8F4 with amber — no leader
  lives anywhere near this register; on a shelf of saturated
  tiles, the cream board is the odd one out by lightness
  alone, before any mark is read.
- **Geometry:** the shelf is peaks, barbells and badges
  (sharp = performance, per the research's pattern 6:
  sharp beats round in performance categories, round beats
  sharp in care/kindness ones). Fit is deliberately in the
  care/kindness geometry — round type (Baloo 2), an oval
  pebble mascot — because its feeling is "a friend who is
  not a coach," not performance. The one leader whose
  geometry is human (MyFitnessPal's jumper) is also the
  revenue king, which is the shelf's own evidence that the
  human lane pays.
- **Colour ownership:** orange (Strava), royal blue
  (MyFitnessPal), magenta (Fitbod) are claimed by giants.
  Fit's amber-on-cream with a stone-grey character is
  unclaimed territory in this category.
- **The fence the research sets (its Fit note, verbatim):**
  "the character must reduce to ONE bold silhouette, or it
  becomes Winnie." Moss already satisfies it: at 32px he is
  one grey oval + one amber stripe (his design pack's read),
  which is one silhouette with one accent — the same shape
  discipline as the leaders' single-symbol marks.

## Verdict for the record

No shelf leader occupies Fit's register (warm cream,
amber, round, character-led, kind). The board's world is
ownable on this shelf, and the category's best commercial
evidence (the human lane's revenue king) supports the lane
the board chose. Condition discharged; this test joins the
board's record ahead of any re-skin build.
