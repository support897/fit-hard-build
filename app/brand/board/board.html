Body · Karla

Tell Moss how today feels, and the day shapes itself around it. A rough day lightens the gym, softens the plan, and keeps a little treat room aside. Nothing is ever lost, and nothing is ever a telling-off.

The icon set · one sprite, area tints

Hand-built SVG, one stroke system, tinted by area. No emoji, no AI art, no gradients.

Moss · design of record

Grey oval, one amber stripe. He never changes shape — the friend does not change.

Contrast · script-computed, fenced honestly

Ink on Cream 14.54 (AAA, all body text) · Ink on Amber 7.65 · Ink on Moss Stone 8.12 · Ink on Teal 4.55 (AA). Area colours on cream run 3.20 to 4.03, so they carry headings, icons and chips at AA-large, never small text. White on area colours is large-bold chips only.

The claim · fonts

Baloo 2 (display) + Karla (body). Google Fonts, Open Font Licence, embeddable and self-hostable. Rounded and humanist — no serif (two siblings are serif-led), no geometric sans, and neither family is used by any other app in the program.
