# Fit App Board v2 — Aisle Test

Evidence source: Store Review's logo and shelf research,
~/workspace/store-review/LOGO-RESEARCH-2026-10-14.md
(Category 7 — Fitness tracking; live store artwork and
chart data, Oct 2026), plus the Fit audience research at
~/workspace/fit-app/brand/FIT-AUDIENCE-RESEARCH.md.

## The shelf

- MyFitnessPal: royal blue, white jumping person, the
  category revenue leader.
- Strava: saturated orange, white peak, performance lane.
- Fitbod: magenta, badge mark, serious training lane.
- Strong / Hevy and the gym crowd: barbells, dark chrome,
  hard geometry.

## Board v2 against it

- **Ground:** the leaders sit on saturated blue, orange,
  magenta, or dark performance chrome. Board v2 sits on
  warm Burrow Cream #FAEED6 with sage. No leader lives in
  this light, quiet register.
- **Lead colour:** one calm sage system replaces the old
  four area hues. Sage is used as deep functional lead,
  mist and tint; Wally Taupe stays with the character.
- **Geometry:** the shelf is peaks, barbells and badges.
  Board v2 is round and low: burrow arc, round companion,
  rounded type forms. That matches the care/kindness lane
  the audience research supports.
- **Character:** the shelf's strongest commercial mark is
  human and warm (MyFitnessPal's jumper). Wally is a
  companion, not a performance symbol; at small size he
  reduces to a round body, two ears and the burrow arc.
- **Audience fit:** the research behind v2 says the
  intimidated majority is not moved by sports energy and
  is put off by judgement cues. A cream and sage burrow
  world is deliberately unlike the performance shelf.

## Verdict for the record

Board v2 is ownable on the fitness shelf by lightness,
calm and companion shape. It does not borrow a leader's
colour, geometry or energy.
